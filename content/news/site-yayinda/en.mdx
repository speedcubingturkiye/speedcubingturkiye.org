The Speedcubing Türkiye website is live. What you will find:

- **[Competition calendar](/yarismalar):** every WCA competition in Türkiye with registration status and capacity; the data comes straight from the WCA.
- **[My first competition](/yarismalar/ilk-yarismam), [FAQ](/yarismalar/sss) and [For parents](/yarismalar/ebeveynler-icin):** everything you need to prepare for a first competition.
- **[Rankings](/siralamalar):** Turkish rankings and national records in 17 events, with WCA profile links.
- **[About us](/organizasyon):** our mission, bylaws, safe environment principles and the filming notice.

When a new WCA competition is announced, it is published here as news and emailed to newsletter subscribers. You can subscribe at the bottom of the page.

The site is bilingual; use the TR switch at the top for the Turkish version of any page. If you spot an error or have a suggestion, write to us via the [contact](/iletisim) page.
