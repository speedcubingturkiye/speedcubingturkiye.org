## Logo package

You may use the Speedcubing Türkiye logo in news, competition posters and community content, following the rules below.

|---|---|
| [logo-long.svg](/brand/logo-long.svg) · [PNG](/brand/logo-long.png) | Horizontal logo: headers, posters, web |
| [logo-mark.svg](/brand/logo-mark.svg) · [PNG](/brand/logo-mark.png) | Logomark: profile pictures, small spaces |
| [logo-long-inverted.svg](/brand/logo-long-inverted.svg) | Horizontal logo, white: dark and red backgrounds |

Colours: red **#E30A17**, black **#1A1A1A**. Ask by email for CMYK and AI files for print.

## Usage rules

- Do not recolour, rotate, squeeze the logo, or add shadows or frames.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least half the logomark's height; do not display it smaller than 24 pixels.
- Use the standard version on white and the white (inverted) version on dark and red backgrounds.
- Do not use the logo in a way that suggests Speedcubing Türkiye organises, supports or endorses an event or product. For joint events and sponsorship, write to us first.
- Speedcubing Türkiye is not yet a Regional Organization recognised by the WCA and does not speak for the WCA. The WCA logo is not used on this site; WCA pages are linked with text links only.
