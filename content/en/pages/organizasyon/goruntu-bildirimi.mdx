Photos and videos are taken at WCA competitions announced by Speedcubing Türkiye and staffed by its volunteers. By entering the venue you acknowledge this notice.

**Who films?** Community volunteers and the organisation team; occasionally invited media.

**What for?** To document the competition and promote the community. Images may be published on this website, on Speedcubing Türkiye social media accounts and in our email newsletter.

**Who may appear?** Competitors, spectators and staff. Solving stations, the podium ceremony and general venue shots may include anyone present.

**If you do not want to be filmed:** Tell the registration desk on the day; our photographers will be informed and will not film you. You may ask us to remove a published image at any time by writing to <DataController field="email" />; we remove it within 30 days at the latest.

**Competitors under 18:** WCA regulations require parent/guardian consent for competitors under 18 to register; please share this notice with your parent or guardian before the competition. Parents: if you do not want images of your child published, tell the registration desk or email us. No reason is needed.

**Filming yourself:** Personal, non-commercial photos and video recordings are allowed. Do not use flash, stay at least 1.5 m away from solving stations and make sure the competitor cannot see your screen. Recording your own solves is subject to WCA regulations; ask your judge. Media and commercial requests: <DataController field="email" />.

**Your personal data:** Your image is personal data under Turkish data protection law (KVKK). The data controller, purposes, legal basis and your rights are set out in our [Privacy Notice](/kvkk).
