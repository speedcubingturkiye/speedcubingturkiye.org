## 2. Which data we process, for what purpose and on which legal basis

### Contact form

- **Data:** name and surname, email address, the subject you chose, your message, the time the form was sent.
- **Purpose:** to answer your request; to assess your volunteer application and contact you.
- **Legal basis:** KVKK Art. 5/2-f: processing is necessary for the legitimate interest of the community in answering your request, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms.
- **Retention:** at most 1 year after your request is closed; volunteer applications for the duration of the role.

### Membership

- **Data:** first name, surname, email address, date of birth, city; your WCA ID if you sign up with your WCA account or link it; optionally, whether you would like to help at competitions; if you are under 18, your declaration of parental or guardian consent; the times of the application and of the confirmation, and the form's language; if you link your WCA account, when you linked it.
- **Purpose:** to establish and maintain membership under Bylaws Article 4 and Motion 04.2026.01 Membership, to keep the list of members and to send members announcements. The date of birth is used to tell whether parental consent is needed.
- **Legal basis:** KVKK Art. 5/2-c: the processing is directly related to establishing and maintaining the membership.
- **Access:** the Secretary and the officers whose work needs it.
- **Retention:** applications not confirmed through the emailed link within 7 days are deleted. The data is kept while the membership lasts and deleted within 30 days after it ends.
- **My account:** on the [My account](/hesabim) page you can see and correct your details, change your email address, link your WCA account, join and leave the province rankings, and leave the membership; the page opens through a link sent to your email, valid for one hour. If you change your address, your newsletter subscription moves to the new one too. When you leave, your details are deleted at once. You can also ask for any of this by writing to <DataController field="email" />.
- **Signing up with WCA:** if you sign up with WCA on the membership page, we receive your name, email address, date of birth and WCA ID from the WCA; you approve this on the WCA's consent screen. They reach the page through an encrypted link valid for 30 minutes, to fill in the form, and are not kept unless you sign up. As the WCA has verified your email address, if you keep it the membership starts at once without a confirmation email; if you change it, a confirmation email goes to the new address.
- **WCA link:** if you link your WCA account in My account, you go to the WCA's sign-in page. The WCA gives us your public profile; we keep only your WCA ID from it. We never see your WCA password. A WCA ID can be verified on one membership only.

### Province rankings

- **Data:** your WCA ID, the city on your membership, when you joined the rankings and the start date of the city rule.
- **Published:** when your province is chosen on the rankings pages, your name and results from the WCA appear. They are already public on the WCA; what is new is your province.
- **Purpose:** to offer members rankings by province.
- **Legal basis:** KVKK Art. 5/1, explicit consent. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian consents too.
- **Withdrawal:** with "Leave the rankings" in My account, or by writing to <DataController field="email" />. The list updates within an hour. Withdrawing does not affect your membership.
- **City:** once you join, your city stays fixed for a year; if you move, write to us.
- **Retention:** leaving deletes the record of joining. The start date of the city rule stays until its year is over.

### Photos and videos at competitions

- **Data:** your image and, where applicable, your voice in photos and videos taken at the competition venue.
- **Purpose:** to document competitions and promote the community; to publish on our website, our social media accounts and our newsletter.
- **Legal basis:** KVKK Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest). You can tell us on the day that you do not want to be filmed, and you can ask for a published image to be removed at any time. Details: [Notice of filming and photography](/goruntu-bildirimi).

### Safe environment reports

- **Data:** the reporting person's name and contact details, the date and a short summary of the report, the names of people mentioned in it, the action taken.
- **Purpose:** to assess the report and take the necessary steps under the principles on the [Safe environment](/guvenli-ortam) page.
- **Legal basis:** KVKK Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest); Art. 5/2-b where life is in danger.
- **Access:** only the Safeguarding Officer, the Deputy and those taking part in an investigation.
- **Retention:** report and investigation files are kept for 5 years after the decision, then deleted.

### Visiting the site

- This site sets only one cookie: a 10-minute, **strictly necessary** one while you sign up with WCA or link a WCA account in My account. Vercel Web Analytics, which we use for visit statistics, sets no cookies, does not store your IP address and does not identify you as a person; it only produces aggregate page view data (page address, referring site, country/city, browser and device type). See the [Cookie Policy](/cerez-politikasi).
- Our hosting provider Vercel may keep short-lived technical logs of requests (including the IP address) for the security of the service and debugging. **Legal basis:** KVKK Art. 5/2-f.

## 3. To whom is your data transferred?

- **Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES):** delivery of contact form messages to us by email, sending membership confirmation emails, keeping the newsletter subscriber list and sending the newsletter. The data is processed in AWS's Northern Virginia (USA) data centre.
- **Vercel Inc. (USA):** hosting of the site and visit statistics.
- **Google (Google Workspace):** our email service; form messages and requests sent by email are stored in our mailboxes, and membership applications, the list of members and the list of newsletter subscribers in Google Sheets, in Google's data centres abroad, including the USA.
- **Authorised public institutions:** only when a legal obligation arises.

Because these providers are located abroad, your data is transferred abroad within the scope of KVKK Art. 9. The transfer relies on the safeguards in the providers' data processing agreements and is limited to the purposes above.

## 4. Collection method

Your personal data is collected electronically through the forms on this site, and at competitions by camera.

## 5. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

By applying to the data controller you have the right to:

- learn whether your personal data is processed,
- request information if it has been processed,
- learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
- know the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom your data is transferred,
- request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
- request erasure or destruction of your data under the conditions of KVKK Art. 7,
- request that corrections, erasures and destructions are notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred,
- object to a result against you arising from the analysis of processed data exclusively by automated systems,
- claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

**How to apply:** by emailing <DataController field="email" />. Your application is answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you find the answer insufficient, you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board.

## 6. Notice for the newsletter subscription

- **Data:** your email address; the newsletter language; the time the confirmation email was sent and the time the confirmation was given; the IP address from which the confirmation was given (as the double opt-in record).
- **Purpose:** to send new competition announcements and community news by email. It is used for no other purpose and shared with no one.
- **Legal basis:** your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1). You can use the site without giving consent.
- **Double opt-in:** the subscription does not start until you click the link in the email sent after signing up. If you ticked the box on the membership form, that link is the one in the membership confirmation email, and it confirms both. If you change your address on My account, the confirmation link sent to the new address is also the double opt-in for moving the subscription there. If you sign up with WCA and keep the address the WCA verified, the subscription starts without a confirmation email; the time the box was ticked and the IP address are kept as the record.
- **Withdrawing consent:** at any time via the "unsubscribe" link at the bottom of every email or by writing to <DataController field="email" />. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing up to that point.
- **Transfer:** the list is kept on Amazon SES (Northern Virginia, USA); emails are sent through Amazon SES. So that the Secretary can see it, the list of confirmed subscribers (email address, newsletter language, time of confirmation) is also kept in Google Sheets.
- **Retention:** addresses not confirmed within 7 days are deleted. The subscription lasts until you unsubscribe; your address is then deleted from the list. Addresses that bounce or report a complaint are kept on Amazon SES's suppression list so that no further email is sent to them.

The newsletter checkbox on the newsletter form or on the membership form is your explicit consent statement; this section is the notice given separately from that consent (Communiqué Art. 5/f).
